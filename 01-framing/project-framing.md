# Framing the project

## Build with people who know the domain

Staff the project with people who understand the business well: a Product Owner for the AI plus the data team.

## The framing meetings

- **Meeting 1** — What will the AI actually do? Define the job in plain terms.
- **Meeting 2** — Reformulate the steps and validate them with the business.
- **Meeting 3** — Implementation with the technical team, with a feedback loop back to the business.

## Design goals to commit to from day one

Architect for these from the start; they are not afterthoughts:

- Observability
- Continuous evaluation
- Production monitoring
- Guardrails

## Running projects in parallel

> **Caution:** Running several projects in parallel is risky. Split attention and diverging approaches make each one harder to land.
