# Adoption & change management

A technically successful agent delivers zero value if the people who do the work don't use it. Adoption is not a launch-day afterthought — it is designed in from framing and owned through production. Treat resistance as data, not obstruction.

## Why it makes or breaks value

- Most agent value depends on humans changing how they work; left unmanaged, freed capacity dilutes and the ROI never lands (see [measuring value](../01-framing/measuring-value.md)).
- Only about **28%** of organisations are positioned to turn AI deployment into high-value outcomes — the differentiator is people and culture, not the model ([EY Work Reimagined 2025](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/insights/workforce/work-reimagined-survey)).
- **66%** of AI users get no guidance on how to reinvest the time the agent frees ([BCG](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2025/how-people-can-stay-super-with-gen-ai)).

## Acculturation: bring people along

- **Involve the doers early.** The domain experts who hold the ground truth are also the adopters; co-design beats imposition (see [project framing](../01-framing/project-framing.md)).
- **Name what changes for each role, honestly** — including whether the intent is reallocation, added capacity, or headcount reduction. Trust collapses when the real intent is hidden.
- **Train for the new workflow, not just the tool** — what to delegate, how to check the agent, when to override.
- **Make the human-in-the-loop meaningful.** People adopt what they can correct and trust (see [error handling](../02-design/error-handling.md)).

## A lightweight change framework

Prosci's **ADKAR** is a practical checklist to run before and through rollout: **A**wareness (why this is happening), **D**esire (what's in it for me), **K**nowledge (how to do it), **A**bility (practice in the real workflow), **R**einforcement (keep it from sliding back).

## Signals to watch

- **Adoption rate** — the share of *eligible* tasks actually run through the agent, not just logins.
- **Override / escalation rate** — a live trust-and-quality signal (see [metrics & SRE](../06-operations/metrics-and-sre.md)).
- **Qualitative feedback** from the people doing the work — surface friction fast and act on it visibly.
